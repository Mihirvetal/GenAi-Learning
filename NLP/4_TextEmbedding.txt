The Perfect End-to-End NLP Pipeline Format
Here is your framework organized into a clean, professional, and structured architectural format that you can save directly into your study notes.

Stage 1: Data Acquisition
Dataset: Gathering raw, unorganized text data (e.g., CSV files, scraped tweets, customer reviews, or a text corpus).

Stage 2: Text Cleaning & Tokenization (Preprocessing I)
Lowercasing: Converting all text to lowercase to ensure consistency (e.g., matching "Machine" and "machine").

Regular Expressions (Regex): Stripping out unwanted noise like HTML tags, URLs, special characters, and punctuation symbols.

Tokenization: Splitting the remaining clean sentences into individual units or words (tokens).

Stage 3: Morphological Pruning (Preprocessing II)
Stopwords Removal: Eliminating high-frequency words that lack unique semantic meaning (e.g., "is", "the", "and").

Stemming: Applying crude, fast rules to chop off prefixes/suffixes to find the root form (e.g., using PorterStemmer).

Lemmatization: Mapping remaining words back to their actual dictionary definition using a database like WordNet (e.g., using WordNetLemmatizer).

Stage 4: Mathematical Feature Extraction (Text to Vectors)
One-Hot Encoding (OHE): Creating binary vectors where a 1 represents the presence of a word in a static vocabulary index.

Bag of Words (BoW): Counting the exact frequencies of words across documents, ignoring sequence or grammatical structure.

TF-IDF: Normalizing word counts by how rare or unique they are across the whole dataset to highlight crucial keywords.

Word2Vec: Processing words through a shallow neural network to assign dense coordinates where similar meanings group together.

Avg-Word2Vec: Averaging out all individual word vectors in a sentence to create one single vector representing the entire sentence.

Stage 5: Downstream Modeling
Supervised ML Algorithms: Feeding the finalized numeric vectors into traditional algorithms (like Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, Random Forest, or Support Vector Machines) to make predictions like classification, sentiment scoring, or spam detection.
